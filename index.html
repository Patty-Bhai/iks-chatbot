📜 Indian Logic (Nyaya Tarka)

1
Observation (Pratyaksha): Empirical perception of the discarded object and condition.
2
Evidence (Hetu): Physical/chemical ground, organic origin, or polymer composition.
3
Inference (Anumana): Universal deduction connecting material nature to biodegradation or recovery.
4
Conclusion (Nigamana): Direct bin assignment and responsible campus disposal action.
