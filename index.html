📜 Indian Logic (Nyaya Tarka)

WasteWise analyzes physical objects and waste images using the classical four-step epistemic method:

1
Observation (Pratyaksha): Empirical identification of the material and physical state (text or photo).
2
Evidence (Hetu): Material ground, organic origin, chemical toxicity, or polymer code.
3
Inference (Anumana): Universal deduction connecting material nature to biodegradation or recovery.
4
Conclusion (Nigamana): Bin assignment, treatment, and safe campus disposal action.

🏷️ Waste Streams & Bins

🟢 Wet Waste: Food leftovers, fruit/veg peels, tea leaves, compostable organic biomass.
🔵 Dry Waste: Clean paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, metal cans, glass jars.
🟣 E-Waste: Phones, chargers, wires, circuits, earphones, batteries.
🔴 Special Waste: Sharp broken glass, chemicals, paint, medical items.
